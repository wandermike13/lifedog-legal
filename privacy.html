Life Dog privacy policy

Last updated: July 2026

Life Dog ("we", "our", or "the App") is designed with privacy as a foundational principle. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Data We Collect

Data you provide

  • Account information: Display name (optional), email (for account creation via sign-in)
  • User preferences: Language, theme, driver mode setting, role (individual/parent/employer/school admin)

Data the app collects automatically

  • Movement session data: Movement mode (walking/jogging/cycling/driving/passenger), duration, distance, average speed, maximum speed, start/end time
  • Screen interaction events: Timestamps of screen-on and screen-off events during active sessions, speed at the time of each event, whether an intervention was triggered
  • GPS data: Location coordinates during active tracking sessions only. GPS paths are encrypted at rest and never shared in raw form on any social or public surface
  • Streak and achievement data: Daily streak counts, achievement unlocks, points totals
  • Device information: Device type, app version, whether the device is managed by an organization

Data we do NOT collect

  • We do not read your SMS messages, call logs, contacts, or browsing history
  • We do not access your camera or microphone
  • We do not track your location when the app is not in an active session
  • We do not sell your personal data to any third party

2. How We Use Your Data

Core app functionality

  • Track your movement sessions and screen-off behavior
  • Calculate points, streaks, and safety scores
  • Display leaderboards and achievement progress
  • Trigger safety interventions (warnings, overlays, vibration)

Safety

  • Escalate high-risk behavior (3+ screen-on events above 30 km/h) to your designated safety contact
  • Generate aggregate, anonymized safety reports for oversight accounts (parents, employers, schools)

Improvement

  • Analyze aggregate, de-identified patterns to improve the app's detection algorithms and intervention effectiveness

Advertising

  • Free-tier users see ads served via Google AdMob
  • Ads are never shown during active tracking sessions (safety-critical restriction)
  • Minors (under 18) see non-personalized ads only
  • Ad-free users ($2.99 one-time purchase) see zero ads app-wide

3. Data Sharing

Who we share with

  • Your designated safety contacts: If you (or your oversight account) configure a safety contact, they receive high-risk alerts. They do not receive your full location history.
  • Oversight accounts (with consent): Parents, school administrators, and employers can view aggregate safety data for monitored users only after explicit consent is verified in the app. Monitoring requires a verified oversight relationship.
  • Partner APIs (insurance, institutional): Only anonymized and aggregated safety scores and metrics are shared. We never transmit personally identifiable information (name, precise GPS, user ID) to partners without your explicit consent recorded in the consent log.

Who we do NOT share with

  • We do not share your data with social media platforms (unless you explicitly use the share feature)
  • We do not share your data with data brokers
  • We do not share precise location data on any leaderboard or social feature

4. Data Protection

  • Encryption: GPS path data is encrypted at rest using industry-standard encryption
  • Minors: Users under 18 appear on leaderboards and social features as first name + last initial only (e.g., "Jamie T."). Precise location and route history are never shared for minors outside the verified parent/guardian chain.
  • Consent verification: All oversight relationships require verified consent. If consent is revoked, data access is blocked immediately.
  • Managed devices: On employer-issued or school-issued devices, monitoring configuration is managed by the organization. Users on managed devices should contact their administrator for policy details.

5. Your Rights

You can:

  • Access your data: View all your session history, screen events, and achievements in the app
  • Delete your data: Sign out and clear all local data from Settings. Contact us at privacy@lifedog.app to request complete deletion of your server-side data
  • Revoke consent: If you are monitored by an oversight account, you can revoke consent at any time. The oversight account will immediately lose access to your data.
  • Disable tracking: Simply don't start a session. The app does not track you passively.

Data retention

  • Active session data is retained for the lifetime of your account
  • You can request complete deletion at any time via privacy@lifedog.app
  • Aggregate, anonymized data (used for improving detection algorithms) is retained indefinitely as it cannot be traced back to you

We take special care with minors: - No precise location or route history shared outside verified parent/guardian chain - First name + last initial only on social surfaces - Non-personalized ads only (regardless of consent status) - No behavioral advertising profiling

We may update this policy as the app evolves. We will notify users of material changes via in-app notification. The latest version will always be available at https://lifedog.app/privacy.
