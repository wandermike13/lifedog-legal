Delete your Life Dog account

Two ways to do it. Neither one costs anything.

In the app, immediately

  1. Open Life Dog.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Under Account, tap Delete account and data.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm.

Your movement sessions, screen events, rewards, achievements and leaderboard entries are erased at that moment, and the account is closed. There is no waiting period and nothing to undo.

By email, if you cannot open the app

Write to wandermike@gmail.com with the subject line: Delete my Life Dog account.

Requests are actioned within 30 days, usually much sooner.

What gets deleted

  • Every movement session: mode, distance, speed, duration and timestamps.
  • Every screen-on and screen-off event recorded during a session.
  • Points, streaks, rewards and achievements.
  • Leaderboard entries and social shares.
  • The account itself, including the email address tied to it.

Safety data and the deletion exception

If you are on a managed device, or an oversight account such as a parent or employer holds a verified consent relationship, a safety alert already delivered to that account stays with it. Deleting your account does not retract a safety report someone already received. Nothing new is sent after deletion.

Purchases

If you bought the ad-free unlock, the purchase record belongs to Google Play or the App Store, not to this account. Deleting the account does not refund it, and reinstalling lets you restore it with Restore Purchases.

Privacy policy

Life Dog is a safety tool, not a medical or legal authority.
